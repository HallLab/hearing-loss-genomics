# Provenance

Which files were copied into this folder rather than produced in it, and the verification
that they arrived intact. Generated by `scripts_provenance.py` reading the disk.

**Commit at generation:** `e977606`

SHA-256 over the first 64 MB. The artifacts SAIGE consumes were additionally compared byte for
byte with `cmp`. MD5 is unavailable on this host, which runs in FIPS mode. Logs and bulk
intermediates are excluded; what is listed is what a reader would want to trust.

**`annotated` is not a failure.** Two inherited FINDINGS files carry a banner added here, saying
they describe the replication's audit rather than this analysis. Content below the banner is
untouched.
